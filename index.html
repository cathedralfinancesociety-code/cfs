NGO Outreach Initiative
PROJECT
SANCHAY
Teaching financial literacy to children with disabilities. Investing in minds, building futures.
From Our Sessions
The Mission

Project Sanchay is CFS's NGO initiative where we teach financial literacy to children with disabilities. Through interactive sessions and hands-on activities, we make concepts like banking, savings, and digital payments accessible and engaging.

We believe financial literacy is a right, not a privilege. Our primary partner is the Jai Vakeel Foundation, Mumbai's leading institution for children with intellectual disabilities. We are actively looking to expand our partnerships and reach.

★ Primary Partner
Jai Vakeel Foundation
Mumbai's leading institution for children with intellectual disabilities
Session Topics
Banking
How banks work, accounts, deposits, and the role of banks in daily life
Finance & Savings
Budgeting, goal-setting, and building a financial safety net
Online Payments
UPI, digital wallets, and safe online transactions
Interactive Activities
Games, group exercises, and real-world simulations
Looking to Expand

We're actively seeking new NGO partnerships to bring financial literacy to more children across Mumbai. If your organisation is interested, get in touch.
